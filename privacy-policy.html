Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At StreamScope Guide, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by StreamScope Guide and how we use it.

Log Files & Analytics

StreamScope Guide follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used solely for analyzing trends and administering the site.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, StreamScope Guide uses "cookies" to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and other information.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

StreamScope Guide's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external streaming platforms (such as Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, or YouTube). We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information.
